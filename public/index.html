Every certificate is checked against your requirements

Want the detail? Read the full subcontractor certificate checklist.

General liability limits
Each occurrence and aggregate against what your prime contract requires, commonly $1M / $2M.
Additional insured, ongoing operations
CG 20 10 (or the carrier’s equivalent form) naming your company for work in progress.
Additional insured, completed operations
CG 20 37 or equivalent, which keeps you covered for claims that surface after handover.
Waiver of subrogation
CG 24 04 on general liability, and WC 00 03 13 on workers’ comp, when your contract calls for them.
Workers’ comp and employers’ liability
Active statutory coverage, plus employers’ liability limits, for every crew on site.
Dates, auto and umbrella
Lapsed or soon-to-lapse policies, commercial auto CSL, and umbrella limits where required.

      Anything else, write to support@certiflowapp.com.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            How does the free trial work?
            
      Sign in with Google, choose a plan and add a card on Stripe’s secure page. For 14 days you can send us certificates and get your gap reports. Your card is first charged when the trial ends. Cancel before then from your account and you pay nothing, and you keep any reports we’ve sent.
      
          
      
          
      
            Can we cancel or change plans later?
            
      Yes, any time. Manage billing in your account opens Stripe’s billing page, where you can cancel, switch plans, update your card or download invoices. A cancelled plan runs to the end of the month you’ve paid for.
      
          
      
          
      
            Who reviews the certificates?
            
      Software reads the ACORD 25 and the endorsement pages, and a person checks every result before it goes in your report. Anything unclear is flagged as unclear, not guessed.
      
          
      
          
      
            What happens to the documents we send?
            
      We use them only to prepare your report. We don’t share them, and we delete them when you ask. See our privacy notice.
      
          
      
          
      
            Do our subs or their brokers need to do anything?
            
      Not for the audit. We work from the certificates you already have. The drafted broker notices are yours to send, edit or ignore.
      
          
      
          
      
            Doesn’t our insurance broker already do this?
            
      Your broker looks after your own policies. Checking every sub’s certificate usually falls to your office. Some brokers do offer tracking, but most track expiration dates, not the endorsement pages behind each certificate. If yours already reviews endorsements for every sub, you may not need us, and the free trial is a quick way to find out.
      
          
      
          
      
            We already use Procore or a spreadsheet. Do we have to switch?
            
      No. The audit works from the PDFs you already have, and the report is yours to keep. Keep tracking wherever you track today.
      
          
      
          
      
            Is this insurance or legal advice?
            
      No. CertiFlow checks documents against the requirements you give us. It isn’t an insurance agency, and it doesn’t replace your broker or attorney on coverage questions.
