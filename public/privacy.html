Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

Your account. When you sign in with Google, Google tells us your name, email address and a Google account ID. We don’t see your Google password and don’t get access to your mailbox, contacts or files.

Billing. Card details are entered on Stripe’s secure checkout page and stored by Stripe, our payment processor. We never see or store your card number. From Stripe we keep your plan, trial and subscription status, and the dates your trial ends and your plan renews.

Company details. What you enter on your account page: company name, phone number if you give it, your approximate number of subcontractors, your insurance requirements and any notes. We also record the time, your IP address, country and browser type to stop spam, the link or website that brought you here, and any answers you gave in the certificate self-check.

Documents. If you send us certificates of insurance or endorsement pages, we keep them only to prepare your reports.

How we use it

To run your account and trial, bill your plan, deliver your gap reports and follow up about them. We don’t sell or share your details or documents, and we don’t add you to a marketing list.

Where it’s stored

Account and company details are stored in a Cloudflare database. Billing records are held by Stripe. A copy of each company-details submission may be emailed to us through our email provider so we can reply quickly.

Cookies. Signing in sets one cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, and a short-lived one during the Google sign-in step that protects it from forgery. Both are needed for the account to work. This site uses no advertising or analytics trackers and loads no third-party scripts. Stripe’s checkout and billing pages, and Google’s sign-in page, are run by those companies under their own privacy policies.

The certificate self-check and the broker notice generator run entirely in your browser. The notice generator remembers your company name on your own device so you don’t have to retype it; that never leaves your browser.

Deleting your data

Email support@certiflowapp.com and we’ll delete your account, your details and any documents you sent. Stripe keeps records of past payments for as long as the law requires.
