Privacy notice

Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you request an audit we store the details you type into the form: company name, your name, work email, phone number if you give it, your approximate number of subcontractors, and any notes. We also record the time of the request, your IP address, country and browser type to stop spam, the link or website that brought you here, and any answers you gave in the certificate self-check. The self-check itself sends nothing until you submit the request form.

If you send us certificates of insurance or endorsement pages, we keep them only to prepare your report.

How we use it

Only to arrange and deliver your audit and to follow up about it. We don't sell or share your details or documents, and we don't add you to a marketing list.

Where it's stored

Form submissions are stored in a Cloudflare database. A copy of each request may be emailed to us through our email provider so we can reply quickly. This site uses no cookies, no advertising or analytics trackers, and loads no third-party scripts.

Deleting your data

Email support@certiflowapp.com and we'll delete your details and any documents you sent.
